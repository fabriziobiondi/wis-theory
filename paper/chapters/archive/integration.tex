\chapter[Integration with WIS]{Integration with Weighted Incidence Structures}

The differential-geometric framework developed in Part~III integrates
with the theory of Weighted Incidence Structures through a single
observation: every WIS determines a manifold of posterior distributions,
and the logarithmic factorisation endows this manifold with a canonical
flat Riemannian metric obtained by quotienting the additive gauge
symmetry.  All subsequent results---the Universal Optimality Defect, the
geometric stability estimates, the universal bounds on information
functionals, and the variational structure---are consequences of this
geometric realisation.

The pullback metric is not equivalent to the Fisher information metric:
the former is forced by the encoding combinatorics, the latter by
statistical parameterisation.  Their differences are the subject of
Chapters~26--27 (Comparative Geometry), which show that the UOD
provides information orthogonal to the classical geometric framework of
information theory.
