# Bibliography

The full bibliography is in the [PDF](https://github.com/fabriziobiondi/wis-theory/raw/main/paper/main.pdf) (around 100 references). Key references in the framework include:

- **Apollonian Cell Encoders**: Biondi et al. (2018) — introduced universal optimality.
- **Perfect secrecy**: Shannon (1949) — the classical definition.
- **Wiretap channel**: Wyner (1975), Maurer (1993) — information-theoretic security.
- **Almost independence**: Csiszár (1996) — connections with WIS factorisation.
- **Information geometry**: Amari (2016) — Fisher metric and the simplex geometry.
- **Optimal transport**: Villani (2009) — Wasserstein distance and gradient flows.
- **Bregman divergences**: Bregman (1967) — convex analysis and the Average Hessian Operator.
- **Spectral graph theory**: Chung (1997) — Laplacians on graphs.
