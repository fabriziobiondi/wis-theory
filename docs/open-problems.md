# Open Problems

The research programme is organised into four categories.

## Structural

- **Probabilistic encoders**: extend the theory from deterministic to randomised encoders.
- **Continuous WIS**: generalise from finite to continuous message/ciphertext spaces.
- **Random WIS**: understand the typical UOD for random incidence structures.

## Geometric

- **Curvature**: replace the flat pullback metric with a curved geometry.
- **Spectral theory**: analyse the distribution-dependent posterior Laplacian.
- **Continuous limits**: study gamma-convergence of the UOD to a PDE.

## Algorithmic

- **Optimisation**: minimise the UOD under integrality constraints.
- **Gradient flows**: numerical computation exploiting the Three-Level structure.

## Applications

- **Privacy–utility trade-offs**: parameterised by the UOD.
